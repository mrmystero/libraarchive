The Creator’s Story

LibraArchive started with a straightforward goal: stop locking basic study guides behind paywalls, blurred previews, and mandatory account sign-ups.

What began as a personal folder of organized math and science notes grew into a public open-directory index where any student can locate and read helpful summaries in seconds.

Why LibraArchive Is Free & Open to Everyone

Built as pure static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript with zero frameworks or trackers, every page loads instantly even on restricted school Chromebooks or slow connections.

All metadata lives transparently in resources.json, and documents stream directly via Google Drive so students can read, print, or save copies without friction.

FAQ

Do I need an account to view or download files?

No. Every resource can be viewed in the embedded reader or opened directly in Google Drive without signing in.

What if the embedded preview is blocked on my school Wi-Fi?

Use the Open in Google Drive action link in the table row or top viewer bar to open the file directly in a new tab.

How are new files added to the directory?

Site maintainers append a new entry with slug, title, grade, subject, type, author, and drive_id to resources.json and push to GitHub.
