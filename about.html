About LibraArchive

A quiet, fast, and open study library built to make high-quality student notes accessible without paywalls or clutter.

The Creator’s Story

LibraArchive started with a simple frustration: every time exams rolled around, the clearest study guides were either buried in private group chats or locked behind subscription paywalls asking students to pay just to view a single review sheet.

What began as a personal folder of organized math and science notes grew into a public shelf where any student can find, read, and bookmark helpful summaries in seconds.

Why LibraArchive Is Free & Open to Everyone

Good study explanations shouldn’t depend on a credit card. LibraArchive is built with lightweight static web technology so pages load instantaneously—even on older school Chromebooks or slow Wi-Fi connections.

There are no user accounts to create, no tracking cookies, and no upload quotas. Every guide is hosted openly and indexed in one transparent catalog so students can spend their energy learning instead of clicking through pop-ups.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to read or download study guides?

No. Every resource on LibraArchive can be read right in your browser or opened directly in Google Drive without signing in.

How are submitted resources reviewed before appearing on the site?

Each submission is checked to confirm that the Google Drive link is publicly viewable, the subject and grade tags are accurate, and the material is original student-created study work.

What if the embedded Google Drive preview doesn’t load on my school network?

Some school filters restrict third-party iframe embeds. Every resource page includes an "Open in Google Drive" button right next to the title so you can view or print the file directly.

How do I add a new resource if I am maintaining this site?

Simply add a new JSON object with slug, title, grade, subject, type, author, and drive_id to resources.json and push to GitHub. Cloudflare Pages updates the live site automatically.
