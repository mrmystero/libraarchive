FAQ

Do I need an account to view or download files?

Usually, no. however if you are already signed into your school account, they may have blocked external access so you would need to sign out to view materials.

It says file is too large to preview, what do I do?

Unfortunately, some files are too big to view directly on the website(over 100mb), so you will need to download the textbook. You can safely ignore the "scan for viruses" warning on Google Drive, it automatically does that for every large file.
