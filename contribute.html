Submission Guidelines

  • Google Drive Sharing: Set General Access to "Anyone with the link → Viewer" so the embedded preview loads cleanly.
  • Formats: PDFs, Google Docs, and Google Slides with clear section headings and legible handwriting work best.
  • Original Work: Only submit notes, summaries, or practice sheets you created yourself.
  • Topic Requests: Include the grade level, subject, and specific unit name (e.g., Grade 11 Chemistry: Stoichiometry).
