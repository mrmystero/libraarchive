Quick Submission Guidelines

  • Set Google Drive Permissions: Open your file in Google Drive, click Share, and set General Access to "Anyone with the link → Viewer" so the embedded reader works for everyone.
  • Clean, Readable Formatting: PDFs, Google Docs, and Google Slides with clear headings, labeled diagrams, and legible handwriting work best.
  • Original Student Work Only: Submit notes, summaries, or practice sheets you created yourself. Do not upload copyrighted textbooks or answer keys to active exams.
  • Credit Your Name (or Alias): Let us know how you would like your author byline displayed on the resource card.
  • Requesting a Topic: Include your grade level, subject, and specific unit (e.g., "Grade 11 Chemistry: Stoichiometry") so contributors know what to build next.
